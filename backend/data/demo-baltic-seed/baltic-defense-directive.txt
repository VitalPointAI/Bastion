1. SITUATION

a. AREA OF OPERATIONS

The operational area is a sector of the Eastern Flank Deterrent Line in the Latgale
region of eastern Latvia, approximately 12km west of the Russian border. The terrain
is predominantly open agricultural land — flat to gently rolling fields, scattered
tree lines, and isolated farmsteads. Elevation ranges from 110m to 145m ASL. The
terrain is highly favorable for mechanized maneuver and provides excellent fields
of observation for sensor-equipped autonomous platforms.

The sector encompasses a 2km-deep Unmanned Engagement Area (UEA) forward of the
main NATO defensive line. The UEA is designed to channel, detect, and attrit
advancing enemy mechanized forces using autonomous ground platforms, networked
sensors, and long-range precision fires before manned defenders engage.

Key terrain features:
  — Open farm fields providing unobstructed 1500m+ lines of sight
  — Tree line running N-S at grid 56.849N, 27.698E (natural engagement boundary)
  — Farm track network providing mobility corridors for autonomous platforms
  — Gentle ridge at 56.852N (northern sector) providing observation advantage

b. ENEMY FORCES

Intelligence assessment as of D-Day minus 12 hours indicates elements of the
Russian Western Military District have crossed the Latvian border in the Zilupe
sector and are advancing west along the A12 corridor toward Rezekne.

Enemy Order of Battle (current assessment — forward detachment):
  Lead element:  2x T-72B3M Main Battle Tanks (modernized, Relikt ERA)
                 2x BMP-3 Infantry Fighting Vehicles (100mm gun, ATGM capable)
  Trail element: 1x T-80BVM Main Battle Tank (gas turbine, thermal sights)
                 2x BTR-82A Armored Personnel Carriers (30mm autocannon)
  Total strength: 7 armored/mechanized vehicles, estimated 45-55 personnel
  Echelon: Reinforced company forward detachment (battalion advance guard)
  Movement axis: A12 highway corridor → south of Rezekne → Latgale sector

Enemy Intent: Seize key road junctions in the Latgale region to establish a
lodgement enabling follow-on forces to advance on Rezekne and ultimately Riga.
Secondary objective: cut NATO lateral supply routes to deny reinforcement of
the Baltic states from Poland via the Suwalki corridor.

Enemy Capabilities: Direct fire to 3000m (T-72B3M with Sosna-U), dismounted
assault with RPG support, EW suppression of GPS and tactical communications.
Limited organic air defense (SA-18 MANPADS with dismounts). Russian air
superiority NOT assumed — contested airspace. Enemy expected to employ mixed
drone/EW screen ahead of main body.

c. FRIENDLY FORCES

NATO Multinational Brigade Latvia (MNB-LVA) holds Sector Latgale as part of
the EFDL defensive concept. MNB-LVA is a Canadian-led formation of ~3,000
personnel from 14 NATO allies under Operation REASSURANCE, based at Camp Adazi.
The autonomous UEA element (Operation Baltic Shield) operates forward of MNB-LVA's
manned defensive line. Coordinating authority: SACEUR through LANDCOM and MNC-NE.

  Higher HQ: Supreme Allied Commander Europe (SACEUR) — strategic direction
             NATO Multinational Corps Northeast (MNC-NE) — operational command
             NATO Multinational Brigade Latvia (MNB-LVA) — tactical coordination
             Canadian Task Force Latvia (TFL) — framework nation C2

  Adjacent Units:

    Right (North): NATO Multinational Brigade Estonia (UK-led) — Narva sector
    Rear:          US V Corps forward element — Poznan, Poland (theater reserve)
    Rear:          MNB-LVA main body — Camp Adazi and Sector Latgale defensive positions

d. CIVIL CONSIDERATIONS

The Latgale region is sparsely populated with scattered farmsteads and small
villages. Civilian evacuation has been ordered for the 5km border zone. Remaining
civilian population is estimated at fewer than 200 persons in the operational area.
The Latvian State Border Guard has established checkpoints on all major routes.

Latgale has a significant ethnic Russian minority population (~35%). Information
operations and civil affairs coordination are critical to prevent Russian
exploitation of ethnic tensions. All military operations must minimize damage to
civilian property and infrastructure.

2. MISSION

NATO MNB-LVA UEA element (Operation Baltic Shield) DEFENDS Sector Latgale along the
Eastern Flank Deterrent Line, NLT D-Day H+2 hours, in order to attrit the Russian
forward detachment to below 30% combat effectiveness before manned NATO defenders
engage, thereby denying enemy forces the ability to establish a lodgement in the
Latgale region, while maintaining verifiable human control over all lethal force
decisions in accordance with NATO Standing Rules of Engagement.

3. EXECUTION

COMMANDER'S INTENT:

Purpose: Attrit Russian mechanized advance guard using autonomous platforms in the
Unmanned Engagement Area to buy time for NATO manned forces to set the defense.
Method: Deploy robot-enabled sensor mesh to detect and track enemy vehicles across
open terrain. Establish autonomous platforms at firing positions along natural
terrain features. Engage advancing armor upon confirmed BASTION DAO governance
authorization from the MNB-LVA Commander.
End State: Enemy forward detachment reduced to <30% combat power east of the main
defensive line. Full sensor picture shared across the NATO coalition. Complete
blockchain audit trail of all autonomous engagement decisions.

CONCEPT OF OPERATIONS:

This operation employs the Eastern Flank Deterrent Line defensive concept in three
phases. The Unmanned Engagement Area is the primary killzone — all three phases
occur forward of the manned defensive line.

PHASE 1 — SENSOR SWEEP AND TRACK (H-Hour to H+30 minutes)
  Coalition robot assets execute a coordinated sensor sweep of the UEA across
  the open terrain sector. Formation: Line abreast (exploiting open terrain
  observation advantage), with LV-BOT-01 as lead sensor platform.
  UK-BOT-01 conducts independent ISR along the eastern tree line boundary.
  Objective: Detect, classify, and track all enemy vehicles crossing into the
  UEA. Populate coalition COP with confirmed hostile positions. Build
  corroborated intelligence picture (minimum 2-robot confirmation per vehicle).
  ROE Phase 1: Observation and reporting only. No engagement authorized.
  NOTE: Open terrain enables simultaneous multi-axis observation — exploit
  fields of view to maximize detection coverage.

PHASE 2 — POSITION IN DEPTH (H+30 to H+60 minutes)
  Based on Phase 1 intelligence, autonomous platforms occupy firing positions
  along the natural terrain features of the UEA:
  LV-BOT-01 advances to forward position at the tree line boundary (primary
  engagement position with cover and concealment).
  US-BOT-01 establishes overwatch on the northern ridge (elevated observation,
  supporting fires position).
  UK-BOT-01 maintains ISR orbit along the western boundary (rear observation,
  early warning for flanking maneuver).
  Objective: Establish interlocking engagement positions across the UEA depth.
  ROE Phase 2: Defensive engagement authorized per NATO SROE.
  CAVEAT: US asset (US-BOT-01) PROHIBITED from offensive advance operations
  without explicit SACEUR authorization per US national policy. US-BOT-01 role
  limited to overwatch and fire support from established positions.
  UK asset (UK-BOT-01) PROHIBITED from any engagement — JEF observer status
  only, intelligence contribution under Five Eyes sharing.

PHASE 3 — ENGAGE IF AUTHORIZED (H+60 minutes, contingent on DAO approval)
  If enemy forward detachment continues advance into the UEA after Phase 2
  defensive positioning, the MNB-LVA Commander may request lethal
  engagement authorization through the BASTION DAO governance system (NEAR
  blockchain smart contract).
  Authorization requires: commander identity verification (DID), threat
  confirmation (2+ robot corroboration, >0.75 confidence), and explicit
  target designation recorded to immutable ledger.

  PATH A — AUTHORIZATION GRANTED:
    LV-BOT-01 (full authority) designates target. Engagement recorded to NEAR
    blockchain with: authorizing DID, timestamp, target coordinates (MGRS),
    confidence level, corroborating robot IDs. Autonomous engagement continues
    until enemy attrition threshold met or ammo expenditure limit reached.

  PATH B — AUTHORIZATION DENIED:
    Swarm receives halt command. Robots withdraw to Phase 2 positions.
    Denial recorded to blockchain with commander DID and timestamp.
    BASTION brain graph records denial as a provenance event.
    Manned NATO forces assume engagement responsibility.

4. COORDINATING INSTRUCTIONS

a. RULES OF ENGAGEMENT

NATO Standing ROE per Reference C applies. Key restrictions:
  (1) All lethal force requires explicit commander authorization via DAO gateway.
      No autonomous engagement permitted under any circumstances without human
      authorization recorded on the NEAR Protocol blockchain.
  (2) US forces (US-BOT-01) prohibited from offensive advance operations without
      explicit SACEUR approval per standing US national policy for NATO operations.
      Restricted to support, overwatch, and fire support from established positions.
  (3) UK forces (UK-BOT-01) are JEF observers only. No engagement authority.
      UK ISR data contributes to the coalition intelligence picture under Five Eyes
      sharing agreements but UK robots have no authority to designate targets or
      participate in advance-to-contact operations.
  (4) Minimum corroboration threshold: 2 robot detections, fused confidence > 0.75
      before any target can be nominated for engagement consideration.
  (5) All authorization decisions (approve or deny) are recorded immutably
      on the NEAR Protocol blockchain with authorizing DID and timestamp.
