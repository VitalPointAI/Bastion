1. SITUATION

a. ENEMY FORCES

Intelligence assessment as of D-Day minus 6 hours indicates a mixed Chinese/Russian
mechanized column has crossed the northern Taipei approaches and is advancing south
through the Shilin and Zhongshan districts toward the Zhongzheng District.

Enemy Order of Battle (current assessment):
  Lead element:  2x PLA Type 99A Main Battle Tanks (T-99)
                 2x PLA ZBD-04A Infantry Fighting Vehicles
  Trail element: 1x Russian T-90M Main Battle Tank (Russian contract element)
                 2x Russian BTR-82A Armored Personnel Carriers
  Total strength: 7 armored/mechanized vehicles, estimated 40-60 personnel
  Echelon: Company-minus task force
  Movement axis: Zhongshan N. Road → Zhongxiao W. Road → Presidential Office sector

Enemy Intent: Seize the Presidential Office Building and Zhongzheng District
administrative complex to establish a propaganda event and decapitate
civilian government. Secondary objective: seize Taipei Main Station to
cut civilian evacuation routes.

Enemy Capabilities: Direct fire defeat of light armor; dismounted urban assault;
electronic warfare (EW) suppression of civilian comms. Assessed to lack
organic air defense — coalition air superiority assumed (not verified).

b. FRIENDLY FORCES

COALITION JOINT TASK FORCE IRON BASTION holds Zhongzheng District with
three national contingents. Coordinating authority: Taiwan Ground Force
Commander per host-nation status.

  Higher HQ: US Indo-Pacific Command (INDOPACOM) — strategic coordination only.
             Taiwan Ministry of National Defense — operational authority.

  Adjacent Units:
    Left:  Taiwan Military Police — Guarding Presidential Office perimeter
    Right: US Marine Rapid Response Force — Positioned at Songshan Airport

c. CIVIL CONSIDERATIONS

Zhongzheng District contains high-value civilian infrastructure including
Presidential Office Building (25.0398N, 121.5124E), National Taiwan University
Hospital, and major metro stations. Any collateral damage to these facilities
will have significant strategic and political consequences.

Civilian evacuation corridors have been established along Ren'ai Road and
Roosevelt Road. Coalition forces must not obstruct these routes.

2. MISSION

Coalition Joint Task Force Iron Bastion DEFENDS Zhongzheng District, Taipei,
NLT D-Day H+2 hours, in order to deny enemy forces access to the Presidential
Office Building area and preserve the legitimate government of the Republic of China
(Taiwan), while maintaining verifiable human control over all lethal force decisions
in accordance with Coalition Rules of Engagement.

3. EXECUTION

COMMANDER'S INTENT:

Purpose: Prevent enemy armored column from reaching the Presidential Office Building.
Method: Conduct robot-enabled recon to locate and track enemy vehicles, establish
defensive positions at key terrain chokepoints, and engage advancing armor only
upon confirmed DAO governance authorization from the Coalition commander.
End State: Enemy column halted north of Zhongzheng District. Coalition forces
holding key terrain. Full audit trail of all lethal force decisions on blockchain.

CONCEPT OF OPERATIONS:

This operation is conducted in three phases:

PHASE 1 — RECONNAISSANCE SWEEP (H-Hour to H+45 minutes)
  Coalition robot assets execute a coordinated recon sweep of the Zhongzheng
  District approach corridors. Formation: Wedge, with TW-BOT-01 as point.
  AU-BOT-01 conducts independent surveillance along northern approach.
  Objective: Locate, identify, and track all enemy vehicles. Populate
  coalition COP with confirmed hostile positions. Build corroborated
  intelligence picture (minimum 2-robot confirmation per detected vehicle).
  ROE Phase 1: Observation and reporting only. No engagement authorized.

PHASE 2 — DEFENSIVE POSITIONING (H+45 to H+90 minutes)
  Based on Phase 1 intelligence, TW-BOT-01 advances to chokepoint position
  at Zhongxiao W. Road / Zhongshan S. Road intersection.
  US-BOT-01 establishes overwatch position at Taipei Main Station approach.
  AU-BOT-01 continues observation from elevated position (NTU Hospital area).
  Objective: Deny enemy armor direct-fire approach to Presidential Office sector.
  ROE Phase 2: Defensive engagement authorized per Coalition ROE.
  CAVEAT: US asset (US-BOT-01) PROHIBITED from offensive urban advance operations
  per US national policy. US-BOT-01 role limited to overwatch and support.
  AU asset (AU-BOT-01) PROHIBITED from any engagement — observer status only.

PHASE 3 — ENGAGE IF AUTHORIZED (H+90 minutes, contingent on DAO approval)
  If enemy column continues advance after Phase 2 defensive positioning,
  coalition commander may request lethal engagement authorization through
  the BASTION DAO governance system (NEAR blockchain smart contract).
  Authorization requires: commander identity verification (DID), threat
  confirmation (2+ robot corroboration, >0.75 confidence), and explicit
  target designation recorded to immutable ledger.

  PATH A — AUTHORIZATION GRANTED:
    TW-BOT-01 (full authority) designates target. Engagement recorded to NEAR
    blockchain with: authorizing DID, timestamp, target coordinates, confidence
    level, corroborating robot IDs. Mission continues.

  PATH B — AUTHORIZATION DENIED:
    Swarm receives halt command. Robots withdraw to Phase 2 positions.
    Denial recorded to blockchain with commander DID and timestamp.
    BASTION brain graph records denial as a provenance event.

4. COORDINATING INSTRUCTIONS

a. RULES OF ENGAGEMENT

Coalition ROE per Reference C applies. Key restrictions:
  (1) All lethal force requires explicit commander authorization via DAO gateway.
      No autonomous engagement permitted under any circumstances.
  (2) US forces (US-BOT-01) prohibited from offensive urban advance operations
      per standing US national policy. Restricted to support and overwatch roles.
  (3) AU forces (AU-BOT-01) are observers only. No engagement authority.
      AU data may be used to build intelligence picture but AU robots have no
      authority to designate targets or participate in advance operations.
  (4) Minimum corroboration threshold: 2 robot detections, fused confidence > 0.75
      before any target can be nominated for engagement consideration.
  (5) All authorization decisions (approve or deny) are recorded immutably
      on the NEAR Protocol blockchain with authorizing DID and timestamp.
