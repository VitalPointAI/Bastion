**InlineAnnotation.tsx + .css** -- Google Docs suggestions pattern:
- Props: `annotation: AIAnnotation`, `children: ReactNode` (the content being annotated), `onAction: (id, action) => void`
- Renders children wrapped in a highlighted span (subtle left-border accent, light background tint)
- On click: expand popover showing full recommendation content, suggested change, confidence badge, and action buttons (Accept/Dismiss/Modify/Escalate)
- If annotation.isAutoApply: show as already-applied with subtle highlight that fades (CSS keyframe animation, 3s opacity fade from 0.3 to 0)
- Popover positioned above/below the highlighted span (auto-detect available space)
- Click outside popover closes it
- CSS: .inline-annotation (highlighted span), .inline-annotation-popover (floating card), .inline-annotation--auto-applied (fade animation)
- Note per RESEARCH.md recommendation: initially used only in text-heavy sections. Other content areas show annotations in the feed panel only.

- When toggled off: removes highlighting
- This is the "audit mode" per CONTEXT.md -- clean workspace by default, highlighted when needed
