## Decisions Made

1. **PhaseProgressionBar Navigation**: Made phase steps clickable to allow commanders to jump to any phase for review or re-planning (supports JP 5-0 backward transitions)

2. **Sensitivity Color Coding**: Standardized Critical=red, High=orange, Medium=yellow, Low=green across all components for consistent risk visualization

3. **Gate Satisfaction via Proposals**: Gate satisfaction initiates proposal creation rather than direct state changes, ensuring all governance actions are auditable through DAO

4. **CommanderGuidance Preview**: Added preview section to show exact proposal structure before submission, reducing submission errors and improving transparency

5. **Assumption Modifier UX**: Used checkbox multi-select with scrollable list rather than dropdown for better visibility of all available assumptions
