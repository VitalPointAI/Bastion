## Decisions Made

**Vote Policy Thresholds:**
- PhaseTransition: 2/3 threshold + 2/3 quorum (high bar because it gates all subsequent planning)
- AssumptionAcceptance: 1/2 threshold + 1/2 quorum (lower bar to avoid bottlenecking)
- ProductApproval: 2/3 threshold + 1/2 quorum (significant products require strong consensus)
- RedTeamGate: 1/2 threshold + 1/2 quorum (acknowledge challenges considered, not block)
- CommanderGuidance: Absolute{count:1} (recorded, not voted on)

**Veto Thresholds:**
- PhaseTransition, ProductApproval: 1/3 veto threshold (allow minority to block)
- AssumptionAcceptance, RedTeamGate, CommanderGuidance: No veto

**Execution Flow:**
- FullyDelegated transitions directly to ReadyForExecution (no veto window, no human approval)
- Enhanced audit log: FULLY_DELEGATED_EXECUTION with "No human monitoring required" note
