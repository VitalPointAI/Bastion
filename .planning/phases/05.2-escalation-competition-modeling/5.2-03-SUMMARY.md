## Decisions Made

1. **Herman Kahn escalation ladder as primary framework**: The 44-rung ladder provides granular escalation modeling from "Ostensible Crisis" to "Spasm War", enabling precise threshold identification and de-escalation pathway analysis per JP 3-0 doctrine.

2. **Three escalation type categories**: Vertical (intensity increase), horizontal (geographic spread), cross-domain (domain shift) cover the full spectrum of escalation dynamics observed in modern conflict theory.

3. **Nuclear threshold awareness**: Each EscalationRung includes nuclearRelevant boolean to flag rungs where nuclear/WMD considerations apply, critical for MDMP Phase 4 wargaming.

4. **maxAutonomy: SemiAutonomous**: Escalation modeling can be performed autonomously, but escalation-sensitive decisions (COA selection involving high escalation risk) require human approval per safety matrix.
