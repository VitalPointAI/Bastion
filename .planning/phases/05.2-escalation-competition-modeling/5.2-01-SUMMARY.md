## Decisions Made

**1. ATP 2-01.3 doctrine as foundation**
- Rationale: Adversary COA development follows established military intelligence doctrine for consistency with MDMP Phase 2 (Mission Analysis)

**2. SemiAutonomous max autonomy**
- Rationale: Agent can autonomously synthesize force models and generate COAs, but human approval required for COA acceptance (affects wargaming assumptions)

**3. Separate force modeling from COA generation**
- Rationale: Force model can be updated as intelligence arrives without regenerating all COAs. Enables incremental refinement.

**4. MLCOA vs MDCOA distinction**
- Rationale: MLCOA (Most Likely) guides primary planning; MDCOA (Most Dangerous) ensures branch/sequel planning addresses worst-case scenarios per doctrine

**5. Intelligence gap identification as first-class output**
- Rationale: Surfaces unknown information needs to drive ISR collection planning (critical MDMP Phase 2 output)
