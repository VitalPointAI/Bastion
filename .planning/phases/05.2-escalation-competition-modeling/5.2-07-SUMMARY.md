## Decisions Made

**COFM Methodology:**
- Chose correlation of forces and means over simple head count to account for combat power factors
- Category weighting (e.g., armor 1.5-2.0x, infantry 1.0x) reflects doctrinal combat power differences
- Seven combat power modifiers capture training, technology, morale, terrain, logistics, intelligence, leadership
- Modifier effect scaling: sum of modifiers (-7 to +7) maps to 0.5x to 1.5x multiplier on base ratio

**Doctrinal Thresholds from FM 3-90-1:**
- Attack (Hasty/Deliberate): 3:1 ratio
- Defense (Prepared): 1:1 ratio
- Defense (Hasty): 1.5:1 ratio
- Delay: 0.5:1 ratio (can delay with inferior force)
- Exploitation: 4:1 ratio
- Pursuit: 3:1 ratio

**Assessment Tiers:**
- Favorable: ≥3:1 ratio (meets attack threshold)
- Marginal: 1.5:1 to 3:1 (meets hasty defense, below attack)
- Unfavorable: <1.5:1 (below defensive thresholds)

**Dual Display Design:**
- Side-by-side horizontal bars for quick visual comparison per CONTEXT requirement
- Friendly forces in blue (left), adversary forces in red (right)
- Category labels between bars for clear reference
- Overall ratio as large prominent number at top
- Doctrinal thresholds shown as indicators with met/unmet status
- Toggle button reveals detailed numeric table on demand per CONTEXT requirement
- Detail table includes: Category, Friendly, Adversary, Unit, Raw Ratio, Weighted Ratio, Assessment
- Threshold assessments table shows all FM 3-90-1 operation types with gap analysis
