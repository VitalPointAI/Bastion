  <action>
Write the Introduction section (~4-6 pages, 1500-2500 words) following the Stanford five-point framework:

**1. What is the problem?** (~2-3 paragraphs)
- Siloed decision-making in coalition operations (from proposal problem statement)
- Fragmented and stove-piped decision cycles
- Gap between strategic intent and tactical execution at operational level
- National policies and caveats constraining intelligence sharing

**2. Why is it important?** (~2-3 paragraphs)
- Degraded operational tempo in coalition operations
- Limited coordinated action across national boundaries
- Threat to achieving strategic objectives in contested environments
- Real-world impact: reference Russia/Ukraine conflict logistics challenges

**3. Why is it hard?** (~2-3 paragraphs)
- Security, trust, and speed friction points (from proposal)
- Centralized C2 vulnerable to disruption
- Poor visibility into available assets
- DDIL (disconnected, intermittent, limited bandwidth) environments
- Multi-national caveats and release authorities

**4. Why haven't previous approaches succeeded?** (~2-3 paragraphs)
- JADC2 connects sensors but lacks decentralized governance
- NATO FMN uses centralized trust model
- Military AI provides decision support but lacks policy compliance framework
- Commercial DAOs designed for business, not C2 tempo
- (Set up for gap analysis in Related Work section)

**5. What are the key components of your approach?** (~3-4 paragraphs)
- AI-augmented DAOs for governance and coordination
- Blockchain for transparency, immutability, and trust across boundaries
- Smart contracts encoding policy constraints and national caveats
- Physical demonstration proving concept (Jetson Orin Nano, Sphero RVR+)
- Three human authority positions: in-the-loop, on-the-loop, out-of-the-loop

**End with explicit research question:**
> How can interconnected, AI-augmented Decentralized Autonomous Organizations (DAOs) provide a secure, transparent, and resilient governance framework that enables effective C2, accelerates decision-making, optimizes resource management, and supports autonomous, policy-compliant coordination across diverse national and organizational boundaries?

**Contribution summary** (bullet list):
- Novel integration of DAO governance with military C2
- AI agent augmentation of decentralized decision processes
- Multi-level coordination (strategic, operational, tactical)
- Physical proof-of-concept demonstration

**Style notes:**
- Active voice throughout
- Define terms on first use (DAO, C2, JADC2)
- No code or implementation details
- Include [CITATION NEEDED] placeholders for claims that need sources
- Consistent terminology (don't alternate between terms for same concept)
