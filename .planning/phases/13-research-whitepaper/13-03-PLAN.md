## 2.9 Human-Machine Teaming

**Authority Positions** (~4-5 paragraphs - important for thesis)
Define clearly as these are central to BASTION demonstration:

1. **Human-in-the-Loop**: Human must approve each AI action before execution
   - Maximum control, slower tempo
   - Required for high-consequence decisions

2. **Human-on-the-Loop**: AI acts autonomously, human monitors with veto capability
   - Balanced control and speed
   - Human can intervene but doesn't have to

3. **Human-out-of-the-Loop**: AI acts fully autonomously within policy constraints
   - Maximum speed, minimum human involvement
   - Appropriate only for low-risk, well-constrained tasks

**Trust and Autonomy Calibration** (~2-3 paragraphs)
- Trust earned through demonstrated performance
- Graduated autonomy based on consequence assessment
- Mission-specific and agent-specific autonomy levels

## 2.10 Gap: AI Governance Frameworks

**Missing Integration** (~2-3 paragraphs)
- Current AI systems lack decentralized governance
- No framework for multi-stakeholder AI authority
- Coalition AI coordination not addressed
- Policy compliance verification not automated

**Research Opportunity** (~1-2 paragraphs)
- Intersection of DAO governance and AI agents
- Smart contract enforcement of autonomy constraints
- This is BASTION's novel contribution
