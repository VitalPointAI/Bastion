## Abstract

**Problem Statement** (1-2 sentences):
Coalition military operations face coordination challenges including siloed decision-making, fragmented decision cycles, and gaps between strategic intent and tactical execution.

**Approach** (2-3 sentences):
This research explores how AI-augmented Decentralized Autonomous Organizations (DAOs) can provide a governance framework for military command and control. The BASTION platform integrates blockchain-based DAOs with AI agents to enable secure, transparent, and resilient coordination across strategic, operational, and tactical levels.

**Key Innovation** (1-2 sentences):
The novel contribution is the systems integration of DAO governance with AI agent orchestration for military C2, preserving human authority over critical decisions through graduated autonomy levels.

**Results** (2-3 sentences):
A physical demonstration validates the approach through an end-to-end scenario connecting strategic resource allocation, operational AI coordination, and tactical autonomous execution. The demonstration shows three human authority positions (in-the-loop, on-the-loop, out-of-the-loop) while maintaining human control over lethal decisions.

**Conclusion** (1-2 sentences):
This research demonstrates that AI-augmented DAOs can accelerate military decision-making while preserving accountability and human control, opening new possibilities for coalition coordination in contested environments.

**Keywords:** Decentralized Autonomous Organizations, Artificial Intelligence, Military Command and Control, Blockchain, Coalition Operations, Human-Machine Teaming

**Style notes:**
- 150-300 words total
- Standalone (no references to "this paper" or sections)
- Active voice
- No citations in abstract
- Include keywords
