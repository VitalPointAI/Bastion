## Decisions Made

- **Immutable messages**: Messages are never modified after creation - only delivery status is appended for full audit trail
- **Lazy ABAC evaluation**: Authorization checked at delivery time, not publish time, allowing dynamic clearance changes
- **pg-boss for reliability**: Leveraged existing pg-boss infrastructure for guaranteed delivery with retries
- **24-hour default TTL**: Messages expire after 24 hours unless specified otherwise
- **1MB payload limit**: Configurable maximum to prevent abuse
