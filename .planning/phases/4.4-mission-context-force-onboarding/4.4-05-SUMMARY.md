## Decisions Made

1. **Milsymbol rendering approach:** Use canvas-based rendering in useEffect to draw military symbols from SIDC codes
2. **Dual view strategy:** Tree view for hierarchy visualization, matrix view for comprehensive relationship overview
3. **Validation approach:** Warn about cycles but don't prevent creation (users may need temporary cycles during reorganization)
4. **Relationship types:** Support full military doctrine set (COCOM, OPCON, TACON, ADCON, DS, GS, GSR, R)
5. **Date range support:** Allow effectiveFrom/effectiveTo for time-bounded relationships
