# Phase 12.1 Context: Coalition Health Monitoring

## Phase Goal

Real-time coalition partner health monitoring, national caveat tracking, and
coalition gate enforcement. Two new AI agents and smart contract extensions
enable continuous assessment of coalition cohesion.

## Depends On

- **Phase 3: DAO Governance** (complete) - Linkages contract with DAOLinkageManager
- **Phase 5.1: MDMP Governance Integration** - Governance gates, MDMP workflow
- **Phase 12: Coalition & Multi-Tenancy** (or can build independently on existing linkages)

## What This Phase Delivers

### Plans

1. **Coalition Health Agent** - Monitor cohesion, track partner posture changes
2. **Narrative Impact Agent** - Info op impact modeling across audiences
3. **National Caveat Tracking** - Extend linkages contract for caveat management
4. **Coalition Gate Mechanism** - Multi-party approval gates in DAO linkages
5. **Coalition Health Dashboard** - Partner status, caveat conflicts, health metrics
