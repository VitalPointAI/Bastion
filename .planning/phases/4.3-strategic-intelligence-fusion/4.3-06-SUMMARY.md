## Decisions Made

- **Validity base score:** 70 (moderate) when no evidence exists
- **Recency decay:** Evidence weight decays over 90 days (min 20% weight)
- **Alert thresholds:**
  - ±20 points → medium severity
  - ±30 points → high severity
  - Score < 30 → critical alert
- **Evidence linking:** Upsert pattern with unique constraint on (objective_id, event_id)
