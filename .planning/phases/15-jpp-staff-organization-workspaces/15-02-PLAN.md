- `activeRole: string` (default 'commander')
- `collapsedCategories: Set<string>` (default empty — all expanded)

5. Active role gets `.active` class
6. Role button click sets `activeRole` and loads workspace content

Main content area renders `<RoleDashboard>` for the active role.

CSS (`StaffWorkspace.css`): Extend TabLayout CSS patterns. Style category headers with subtle background, bold text, chevron rotation on collapse. Indent role buttons under categories. Maintain the sidebar collapsed/expanded toggle.
