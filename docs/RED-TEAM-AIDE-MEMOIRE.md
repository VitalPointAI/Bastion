# TEAM SYNCHRONIZATION GUIDE

## How the Positions Work Together in BASTION

### Competition Phase — Unified Effort
Everyone works together in the **Understand** and **Design** tabs. The JPG Lead facilitates, Regional Planners feed zone assessments, advisors contribute their instrument of power. The goal is a shared understanding and initial problem framing.

**Team workflow:**
1. All members upload documents to **Understand** tab (organized by zone/topic)
2. JPG Lead facilitates **Design > Problem Framing** (all contribute)
3. JPG Lead assigns **CoG Analysis** working groups
4. Team reviews AI-generated COP for accuracy

### Crisis Phase — Red Team Planning
The team shifts to operational planning. The JPG Lead drives, CJ 35 builds COAs, Theater LNOs and service planners feed component capabilities, Enablers provide intel and logistics, Diplomacy advises on escalation.

**Team workflow:**
1. JPG Lead runs the planning cycle from **Design > Operational Approach**
2. CJ 35 Planner develops COAs in **Plan** tab
3. Theater LNOs + service planners contribute component plans
4. Intel/Enablers produce intelligence estimates and enabler annexes
5. DoS Rep adds diplomatic dimension to each COA
6. Team war-games COAs together; CJ 35 documents results
7. JPG Lead synthesizes into operational approach narrative (use AI assist)

### Conflict Phase — Execution
Commander decides, CJ 3/5 synchronizes, Subcenters execute in their domains.

**Team workflow:**
1. Commander approves COA via **Direct** tab governance
2. CJ 3/5 manages battle rhythm; publishes FRAGO updates
3. Subcenters (Land, Maritime, Air, Missile) execute their plans in **Plan** tab
4. All subcenters update **COP** tab in their domains
5. Intel/Enablers provide rolling intelligence and logistics updates
6. Strategic Advisor monitors for negotiation opportunities
7. CJ 3/5 briefs Commander; Commander makes decisions at decision points
